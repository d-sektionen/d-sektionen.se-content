Curious about management consulting and passionate about tech?  
BCG Platinion, the tech strategy part of Boston Consulting Group (BCG), invites you  
to their annual DigiTalent Dinner in Stockholm on Thursday, November 12!  
By combining business strategy with cutting-edge technology, BCG Platinion advises  
global clients on their tech agenda, guiding them toward a successful digital future.  
The event kicks off at the BCG Stockholm office with a presentation, including  
insights into daily IT consulting life, international projects, and diverse career  
journeys. This is followed by dinner and drinks at a nearby restaurant, where you will  
have the opportunity to ask questions and connect with both BCG Platinion  
consultants and fellow participants.  
Learn more and apply by Sunday, November 1 to secure your spot:  
[https://on.bcg.com/digitalentdinnerstk](https://on.bcg.com/digitalentdinnerstk) .  
Travel expenses from other parts of Sweden to Stockholm will be covered by BCG  
Platinion.
